# Fan Experience Journey

**Status:** WORKING MODEL / HYPOTHESIS

The fan relationship is year-round. Suite should organize around the moments that matter to the fan rather than around the internal structure of the data platform.

## 1. Discover

**Fan behavior:** Hears about the team, a player, a game, or a story.

**Current friction:** Information is scattered and the fan has to decide where to go next.

**Suite opportunity:** A clear team destination that immediately establishes team identity and the most relevant current information.

**TeamOS need:** Team identity, current state, core content, schedule.

## 2. Follow / Set Up

**Fan behavior:** Chooses a team and decides whether to return or receive updates.

**Current friction:** Following a team often means configuring several unrelated products.

**Suite opportunity:** Simple team follow and personalization.

**TeamOS need:** Stable team identity, capabilities, notification-relevant events.

## 3. Prepare for the Game

**Fan behavior:** Checks when and where the game is, opponent, broadcast, news, injuries/availability, and expectations.

**Current friction:** Preview information lives across schedules, news, social, and media.

**Suite opportunity:** A single pre-game destination with the information that matters most.

**TeamOS need:** Game object, schedule, venue, team/player context, news/media, freshness.

## 4. Game Day

**Fan behavior:** Watches, checks the score, follows updates, reacts, and looks for context.

**Current friction:** Fans switch among score apps, social feeds, broadcasts, and news.

**Suite opportunity:** Game Center with fast live state, key moments, stats, broadcast context, and lightweight participation.

**TeamOS need:** Live game state, normalized stats/events, freshness and provider status.

## 5. Immediately After the Game

**Fan behavior:** Checks the final result, key moments, stats, reactions, and what happens next.

**Current friction:** Recaps, stats, video, and conversation are fragmented.

**Suite opportunity:** A coherent post-game experience that changes from live state to recap state.

**TeamOS need:** Final game state, result, stats, media, next event.

## 6. Between Games

**Fan behavior:** Checks news, roster changes, media, rankings, and the next game.

**Current friction:** The fan has to repeatedly reconstruct what is relevant.

**Suite opportunity:** A useful home surface that answers "what should I know today?"

**TeamOS need:** Current team state, upcoming events, content normalization, freshness.

## 7. Major News / Offseason

**Fan behavior:** Follows recruiting, transactions, coaching news, schedule changes, player movement, and historical context depending on sport.

**Current friction:** The team relationship remains important even when there is no game, but most products are organized around games or broad sports coverage.

**Suite opportunity:** Maintain the team as a year-round destination.

**TeamOS need:** Sport/league capabilities, event model, news/media, roster history, structured context.

## 8. Return Loop

The intended loop is:

> Discover -> Follow -> Customize -> Return for relevant information -> Interact -> Share -> Return

The product should earn repeated use through utility and relevance before relying on monetization.

## Validation Questions

- Which stage creates the strongest activation moment?
- Which stage drives repeat visits?
- Does the home surface reduce the need to visit multiple products?
- What information do fans actually want notifications for?
- Which participation features increase engagement without creating moderation or infrastructure burdens too early?
- Which moments are common across teams and which require sport-specific treatment?
