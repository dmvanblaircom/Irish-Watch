# Product Strategy & Business Model

**Status:** DECISION

## Phase 1 Strategy

Project LND is consumer-first in product development but not consumer-revenue-first.

The initial fan experience should be free by design. The purpose of Phase 1 is to earn the fan relationship, learn from real usage, prove retention, and establish the reusable platform.

> **We don't monetize the fan first. We earn the fan first.**

## Why Free First

Keeping the first fan experience free removes an early barrier to adoption and avoids adding consumer billing, subscription, and cancellation complexity before the product has demonstrated recurring value.

The initial business question is not "How much will a fan pay?"

It is:

> "Will a fan repeatedly choose this experience because it makes following their team better?"

## Long-Term Business Model

The primary long-term commercial opportunity is the platform layer, with additional organization and ecosystem opportunities as the product proves itself.

Potential revenue streams include:

1. **TeamOS SaaS / organization licensing**
   - Team-specific platform capabilities
   - Fan experience infrastructure
   - First-party fan understanding
   - Analytics and engagement capabilities

2. **Sponsorship / activation**
   - Contextual sponsorship opportunities
   - Team and brand activations
   - Measurable engagement

3. **Commerce / ecosystem**
   - Affiliate or partner commerce where it genuinely improves the fan experience

4. **Consumer premium features later**
   - Only where users demonstrate willingness to pay for incremental value

The ordering is intentional but not a promise about future revenue mix.

## Data Principle

Project LND should not sell individual user data.

First-party understanding should be used to improve the fan experience and, where appropriate, create aggregate, permissioned, or privacy-safe value for organizations and partners.

## Strategic Loop

> Engaged fans -> better first-party understanding -> more valuable team platform -> better fan experience -> more engagement

The platform should become more useful as the fan relationship grows.

## Capital Principle

Early spending should be deliberately constrained. The product should prove demand before significant infrastructure, staffing, or acquisition investment is added.

Initial founder cash ceiling for the current phase: **$5,000**.

The desired progression is:

> Founder capital -> working product -> fan validation -> first revenue -> product funds further product

## Business Model Questions To Validate Later

- Which organizations have the strongest pain around fan experience and first-party data?
- What capabilities would organizations actually pay for?
- What is a credible annual TeamOS contract size?
- Which sponsorship opportunities improve rather than degrade the fan experience?
- When, if ever, do consumer premium features make sense?

## Guardrail

Do not allow monetization requirements to distort the initial fan experience before the product has earned the fan relationship.
