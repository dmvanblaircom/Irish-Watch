# Project LND Product Thesis

**Status:** DECISION / working thesis  
**Last updated:** 2026-09-16

## The problem

Following a team has become fragmented. A fan may use one place for schedules, another for scores, another for news, another for social conversation, another for highlights, and another for team information.

The individual products may each do their job well. The problem is the overall fan experience.

## The opportunity

Project LND aims to create a persistent digital home for a fan's relationship with a team.

The goal is not to replace ESPN, social platforms, news sites, streaming services, or official team channels. The goal is to make the relationship with the team easier to follow and more coherent while those services continue to exist.

## Core job to be done

> **Suite helps me stay connected to my team without having to piece my fandom together across a dozen different places.**

## Product thesis

A passionate sports fan will repeatedly use a free, team-specific digital experience when it makes following their team more convenient, relevant, and engaging than the fragmented alternatives they currently use.

This is a HYPOTHESIS, not a proven fact.

## Who we are building for first

The first target is a highly engaged college sports fan whose fandom is part of a weekly routine.

They are likely to:

- know the team's schedule
- watch or follow games
- follow news and recruiting
- care about players and storylines
- consume highlights and stats
- discuss games with other fans
- care about the team between games

Notre Dame is the initial laboratory because Irish Watch already provides a working environment for testing the experience. Notre Dame is not assumed to be the permanent market boundary.

## Product pillars

### Connect

Help fans know what is happening with their team.

Examples: schedule, scores, standings, roster, players, news, results, and upcoming events.

### Experience

Make following the team feel richer than a list of links.

Examples: Game Center, live updates, recaps, highlights, stats, team moments, and personalized content.

### Participate

Give fans lightweight ways to interact.

Examples: reactions, predictions, polls, game-day interaction, discussion, and sharing.

### Belong

Build a persistent relationship between the fan and the team.

Examples: favorite team, favorite players, personalization, notifications, and a consistent team identity.

## The product loop

```text
Discover
   ↓
Follow team
   ↓
Customize
   ↓
Receive relevant information
   ↓
Interact
   ↓
Return
   ↓
Invite / share
   ↓
More fans
   ↓
More activity
   ↓
Better experience
   ↓
Stronger team relationship
```

## Phase 1 philosophy

Phase 1 consumer access is intentionally **free by design**.

The objective is to earn the fan first through product quality, usage, retention, and trust. Consumer billing is not the initial growth mechanism.

> **We don't monetize the fan first. We earn the fan first.**

The longer-term commercial model is expected to focus primarily on TeamOS as a platform for organizations, with potential additional revenue from sponsorship/activation, commerce/ecosystem relationships, and eventually optional consumer features if the evidence supports them.

## What we are deliberately not building first

The initial product should not attempt to become all of the following at once:

- a full social network
- a messaging platform
- a fantasy sports product
- a betting product
- a ticket marketplace
- a merchandise marketplace
- a giant AI assistant
- a broad ad platform
- a complex gamification system
- a paid subscription product

These may become relevant later. They are not required to validate the core thesis.

## Success condition

The strongest early signal is not revenue. It is evidence that fans repeatedly choose Suite because it makes their relationship with the team easier and better.

A particularly valuable expansion signal would be an Irish Watch user asking for the same experience for another team. That would suggest the platform concept, rather than the Notre Dame content alone, is creating value.

## What must remain true

1. The fan experience remains fast and easy to use.
2. Core information is accurate and trustworthy.
3. Personalization improves relevance without creating clutter.
4. Irish Watch remains a laboratory for reusable concepts.
5. TeamOS is developed around capabilities that can support multiple team experiences.
6. Product decisions are validated with real fan behavior whenever practical.
