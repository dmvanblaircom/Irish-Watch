# 0002 — Free Fan Experience in Phase 1

**Status:** Accepted  
**Date:** 2026-09-16

## Context

The first objective of Project LND is to determine whether fans repeatedly value the Suite experience. Introducing consumer billing too early could add operational complexity before product-market fit is established.

## Decision

The Phase 1 fan experience will be free by design.

The primary objective is to earn the fan through product quality, usage, retention, and trust rather than immediate consumer revenue.

> **We don't monetize the fan first. We earn the fan first.**

## Consequences

### Positive

- Lower friction for acquisition and sharing.
- Faster learning from real users.
- No early subscription/refund/chargeback support burden.
- Clear focus on product value before monetization.
- Allows TeamOS to become the primary long-term commercial focus.

### Tradeoffs

- Phase 1 consumer revenue is intentionally $0.
- Infrastructure and operating costs still exist.
- The product must eventually demonstrate a credible path to sustainable revenue.

## Longer-term monetization hypothesis

Potential revenue streams include:

1. TeamOS SaaS for organizations.
2. Sponsorship and partner activation.
3. Commerce and ecosystem relationships.
4. Optional consumer premium capabilities if validated by user demand.

These are hypotheses, not commitments or forecasts.

## Review trigger

Revisit this decision when the product has meaningful evidence of repeat usage and retention, or when a clearly validated consumer feature creates a compelling reason to introduce paid access.
